Overview

The 9th World Dictionary Days focus on the theme “Développement durable, dictionnaires & intelligence artificielle” (Sustainable Development, Dictionaries & Artificial Intelligence, D3IA). They take place on 5 and 6 November 2026 at the École Supérieure des Sciences Économiques et Commerciales de Tunis, Université de Tunis.

Organised within the framework of the French-Tunisian PHC Utique project D3TIP, “Discours du Développement Durable : Traitement Informatique Phraséologique”, the event brings together researchers from France, Tunisia, Italy and the United States to discuss the lexicographic and phraseological proceedings of sustainable development discourse and the impacts of artificial intelligence.

Participation is possible on site or online, upon registration.
