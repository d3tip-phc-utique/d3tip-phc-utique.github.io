Overview

The 9th World Dictionary Days are devoted to the theme “Sustainable development, dictionaries and artificial intelligence” (D3IA). They take place on 5 and 6 November 2026 at the Higher School of Economic and Commercial Sciences of Tunis (ESSECT), University of Tunis.

Organised as part of the French-Tunisian PHC Utique project D3TIP, “Discourse of Sustainable Development: Computational and Phraseological Processing”, the event brings together researchers from France, Tunisia, Italy and the United States to discuss the lexicographic and phraseological description of sustainable development discourse and the contribution of artificial intelligence.

The first day is devoted to papers. The second day also offers two training workshops on corpus processing tools.

Participation is possible on site or online, upon registration.
